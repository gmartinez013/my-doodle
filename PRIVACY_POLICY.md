# Privacy Policy for ColorBot Alexa Skill

**Last Updated: January 24, 2026**

## Overview

ColorBot is an Alexa skill that generates custom coloring pages for children. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

When you use ColorBot, we may collect:

- **Voice Commands**: The subjects you request for coloring pages (e.g., "dinosaur", "unicorn")
- **Email Address**: Your Amazon account email address, used solely to deliver coloring page download links

## How We Use Your Information

- **Image Generation**: Your voice commands are used to generate custom coloring page images
- **Email Delivery**: Your email address is used only to send you links to download your coloring pages
- **No Data Retention**: We do not store your personal information beyond the immediate transaction

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

- **Service Providers**: We use AWS (Amazon Web Services) for hosting and OpenAI for image generation
- **Legal Requirements**: If required by law

## Children's Privacy

ColorBot is designed for use by children under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
- Secure cloud infrastructure (AWS)
- No long-term storage of personal data

## Your Rights

You may:

- Stop using the skill at any time
- Disable the skill through your Alexa app
- Contact us to request deletion of any data

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the skill's support channels.
